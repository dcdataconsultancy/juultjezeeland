Cancellation policy

At Juultje Zeeland we understand that plans sometimes change. That is why the following cancellation policy applies to bookings of our apartment and holiday cottage in Wissenkerke.

1. Cancellation by the guest

The following terms apply to cancellations:

  • 30 days or more before arrival: free cancellation.
  • 14 to 29 days before arrival (inclusive): 50% of the total booking price is charged.
  • Less than 14 days before arrival: 100% of the total booking price is charged.
  • Not arriving (no show): 100% of the total booking price is charged.

2. Early departure, late arrival and personal circumstances

If you leave early or arrive later than the agreed arrival date, unused nights will not be refunded.

Illness, an accident, the death of a family member or other personal circumstances do not automatically entitle you to free cancellation or a refund. We therefore recommend taking out suitable travel insurance and cancellation insurance.

3. Weather conditions and exceptional situations

Normal weather conditions, bad weather or a disappointing forecast do not entitle you to free cancellation or a refund.

If, due to exceptional circumstances such as serious storm damage, flooding, fire or another situation beyond the control of Juultje Zeeland, the accommodation cannot be made available safely or reasonably, we will inform the guest as soon as possible. Where possible, we will offer suitable alternative accommodation. If no suitable alternative is available, the amount already paid for the period not provided will be refunded in full.

4. Cancellation by Juultje Zeeland

If Juultje Zeeland is unable to provide the booked accommodation due to an error by the host, a double booking, a technical failure or another circumstance on the host's side, we will contact the guest as soon as possible and make every effort to offer equivalent alternative accommodation.

If no suitable alternative accommodation is available, or it cannot reasonably be offered, the amount already paid will be refunded in full.

5. No statutory cooling off period

There is no statutory 14 day right of withdrawal for booking accommodation for a specific period.

6. How do I cancel my booking?

You can cancel a booking via:

  • Email to Juultje Zeeland: juultjeverhuur@gmail.com
  • The booking platform where the booking was made, such as Booking.com or Airbnb.

The date on which we receive the cancellation counts as the official cancellation date.

7. Bookings via external platforms

Different cancellation terms may apply to bookings made through Booking.com, Airbnb or other booking platforms. In that case, the cancellation terms agreed on that platform at the time of booking apply.
