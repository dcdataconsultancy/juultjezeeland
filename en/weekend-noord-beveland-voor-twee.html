Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

A weekend away for two on Noord-Beveland

Two days are enough to get to know Noord-Beveland at an easy pace. On day 1 you stay on the island, on your bike and by the water. On day 2 you drive to one of the towns nearby. You stay in Wissenkerke, in a place that is set up for exactly two people.

What the weekend looks like

This is a suggestion, not a strict schedule. Noord-Beveland is flat and easy to get around, so you can swap everything round at your own pace. Keep the weather in mind: in rain or strong wind choose the town, and in nice weather stay outdoors.

You can arrive from 3 pm, and check out is by 10 am at the latest. The minimum stay is 1 night, so even a single night is possible. For this weekend we count on two nights.

Day 1: cycling, water and the island itself

Start the morning slowly in your own place. Then rent electric bikes from us. The first day costs €25 per bike and each following day €15 per bike. With electric assistance the wind on the island is less of an obstacle.

Zeeland has a cycle junction network. A logical choice is the Ontdek Noord-Beveland route of 36 km. It runs past the villages of Kortgene and Colijnsplaat, along cycle paths on the outer side of the dykes. You have views of the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer and pass inlagen full of birds. You can read a fuller overview at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

In Kortgene a stop is worth it. You will find the Delta Marina marina and the windmill De Korenbloem from 1873. If it is Saturday, the mill shop is open and there is a market by the church.

In Colijnsplaat you can see the rebuilt temple of Nehalennia, which is free to visit daily between 11 am and 5 pm. The Roman cargo ship Caudicaria is also there, on show since 4 September 2021.

The beach is about 2.5 km from the places to stay. That is close enough for a short walk at the end of the afternoon. Bring a jacket, because it can be a lot cooler on the open water than in the shelter of the village.

If you would rather be on the water, you can go to the Veerse Meer. Windsurfing is popular there, and there are surf schools where you can learn to surf, paddleboard, wakeboard or waterski.

Day 2: a town nearby

On day 2 you drive to one of the towns in the area. Which suits best depends on what you want to do. The distances below apply by car and are approximate.

Veereapprox. 21 km

Goesapprox. 17 km
Neeltje Jansapprox. 15 km

Veere lies on the Veerse Meer and is where the cycle route around the Veerse Meer passes. That route is about 60 km, so for a weekend it is a big day. In summer a passenger boat sails between Veere and Kamperland. Check the timetable before you count on it.

Zierikzee lies on the Oosterschelde. Seal safaris with Safari Frisia Rondvaarten leave from Zierikzee, lasting about 2 hours and by reservation. To find out whether you can expect seals, read seals in the Oosterschelde.

Middelburg is the furthest of the three, at about 23 km. Choose it if you want to spend a day in a town, and allow some extra travel time.

If you would rather stay close by, you can also go to Neeltje Jans. The boat trip there is only available together with a park visit and usually departs at 1.30 pm. In the storm season, from October to April, the trip may be cancelled.

Season

What is there to do in each season?

Summer

In summer the ferries run: from Veere to Kamperland as a passenger boat and from Kortgene to Wolphaartsdijk as a cycle and foot ferry. Check the timetable beforehand. The Eindeloos Eiland Festival also takes place in Kortgene, on the Galgendijk. We do not know the dates for the coming year, so look at the festival's website.

Autumn and winter

This is a good time to walk, over the dyke and in the area with woods and salt marshes. On paths in nature reserves a closure can apply temporarily, for example because of maintenance or hunting. Always watch the signs.

With a dog

A dog may come along, up to three per place, for €15 per dog per night. In built up areas your dog must be on a lead. Checked in November 2026 via the dog page of the Noord-Beveland municipality. You can read more at holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland.

Stays

Where do you sleep as a couple?

At Juultje Zeeland you choose between two places to stay in Wissenkerke. Both have 1 bedroom with a double bed, 1 bathroom, wifi and air conditioning.

The holiday cottage is about 35 m² and has a small garden with a wooden canopy. You park on your own drive. There are a dishwasher and a washing machine, and a TV with Chromecast and Netflix. The price starts at €86 per night in low season and €130 in high season.

The apartment is about 26 m² and has its own terrace. You park in the street. The price starts at €72 per night in low season and €100 in high season.

Tourist tax is included in both. You can read more about the village at Wissenkerke: things to do in and around the village.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many nights do you need for a weekend away on Noord-Beveland?

With two nights you have one day for the island and one day for a town such as Veere, Zierikzee or Middelburg. Our minimum stay is 1 night, so shorter or longer stays are possible too.

Can you rent an electric bike?

Yes. The first day costs €25 per bike and each following day €15 per bike.

How far are Veere, Zierikzee and Middelburg from Wissenkerke?

By car Veere is about 21 km, Zierikzee about 22 km and Middelburg about 23 km. These distances are approximate.

Is there room for two people and a dog?

Both places are for two people. Dogs are welcome, up to three per place, for €15 per dog per night. They may lie on the sofa and the bed with their own blanket.
