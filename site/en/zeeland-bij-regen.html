Zeeland

Zeeland in the rain: good days out indoors

If it rains in Zeeland, head to the Watersnoodmuseum in Ouwerkerk, the Zeeuws Museum in Middelburg, an oyster farm in Yerseke, or a cinema or swimming pool. All the outings below are within about 35 minutes' drive of Wissenkerke. We do not list prices or opening hours, as they change: check them on the official website.

What do you do in Zeeland when it rains?

Zeeland has a surprising range for bad weather. There are museums with a story of their own, a historic abbey complex, tastings of regional products and simple day trips such as a film or an afternoon swim. It is wise to choose one outing and then end in a café or restaurant nearby.

Showers often pass quickly in Zeeland, because the land is flat and the wind keeps moving them on. If you want to be flexible, check the rain radar an hour beforehand and choose between indoors and outdoors. You will find an overview of all the outings at things to do in Zeeland.

Museums and history

Indoor days out with a story

Watersnoodmuseum, Ouwerkerk

This museum is housed in the caissons that closed the last gap in the dyke at Ouwerkerk in 1953. Each of the four caissons has its own theme. You can take a free audio tour with the WATERgezicht app. Read more at watersnoodmuseum.nl.

Zeeuws Museum, Middelburg

The Zeeuws Museum is in the abbey in Middelburg and tells the story of the culture and history of Zeeland. It is known for its sixteenth century tapestries with sea battles. There is also a fashion exhibition and an exhibition about Zeeland from surprising angles. The programme changes, so check zeeuwsmuseum.nl.

Abbey complex Middelburg

The abbey is more than just the museum: the complex includes the Nieuwe Kerk, the Koorkerk and the Abbey Tower. Whether you can visit the churches and climb the tower varies through the year. Check this in advance via Zeeland.com.

Deltapark Neeltje Jans

This park at the Oosterscheldekering offers, among other things, the VR Experience Onderwaterwereld and an interactive exhibition about how the Oosterschelde came to be. A large part of the park is outdoors, so choose a day with showers rather than a stormy day. The boat trip is only available with a park visit and may be cancelled in bad weather from October to April. Current information is available at neeltjejans.nl.

Tasting, swimming and film

Eating, swimming or watching a film indoors

Tasting oysters in Yerseke

Yerseke lies on Zuid-Beveland and is known for oysters and mussels. De Oesterij has an oyster farm and a page about tastings and visits. You can see which tours and tastings are on now at oesterij.nl. You can read more about regional products at the Zeeland specialities.

Swimming in Goes

Omnium in Goes calls itself the subtropical swimming pool of Zeeland. Besides swimming there are sports halls and a restaurant. It is a good choice for an afternoon with lots of showers. Information is at omnium.nl.

Cinema in Middelburg

Cinema Middelburg has a large screen and a club room and shows a mix of new films and classics. The programme is at cinemamiddelburg.nl.

Tips

How to plan a rainy day

Which indoor outing suits which weather?

In persistent rain choose a museum or the swimming pool, because you can stay there for hours. In changeable weather Neeltje Jans suits, with indoors and outdoors. In light rain a town like Middelburg also works, because you can shelter between shops, terraces and buildings. Read more at the Zeeland towns.

What should you bring?

A rain jacket and sturdy shoes are enough. Many indoor outings are by the water or on open ground, where it blows harder than you might expect. An umbrella is therefore less useful than a jacket with a hood.

And with a dog?

Check in advance at each outing whether dogs are welcome inside, because that differs from place to place and we cannot judge it for you. You can read more about walks and beaches with a dog at holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland.

And in autumn and winter?

In the low season some attractions are open for shorter hours or closed, and storms can cancel outdoor activities. You will find our overview at Zeeland in autumn and winter.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What can you do in Zeeland in bad weather?

You can visit the Watersnoodmuseum in Ouwerkerk, the Zeeuws Museum in Middelburg, Neeltje Jans, a cinema, a swimming pool or an oyster farm in Yerseke.

Are there museums on Noord-Beveland or nearby?

The Watersnoodmuseum is in Ouwerkerk on Schouwen-Duiveland, about half an hour's drive away. The Zeeuws Museum is in Middelburg on Walcheren.

Is Neeltje Jans suitable in the rain?

Partly. Part of the park is indoors and part is outdoors. Check the park's website in advance to see which areas are open.

Where can I swim when it rains?

Omnium in Goes is a covered swimming pool. Check the current opening times and any maintenance work in advance.
