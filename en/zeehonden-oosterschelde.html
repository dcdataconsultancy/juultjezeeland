Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

Seal spotting in the Oosterschelde

You see seals in the Oosterschelde mainly around Neeltje Jans and on a boat trip. From Wissenkerke Neeltje Jans is about 10 km by bike. A seal safari leaves from Zierikzee, among other places.

Seals in the Oosterschelde

The Oosterschelde is one of the places in Zeeland where seals live. Zeeland.com also mentions the Grevelingen, the Westerschelde and the Voordelta, and in addition the area around Neeltje Jans and the beach at Vlissingen.

These are mainly harbour seals. Grey seals are also present.

Seals like to rest on sandbanks that are exposed at low tide.

Besides seals you sometimes see porpoises. Zeeland.com names Wissenkerke itself as a good place to see porpoises from the shore, alongside Colijnsplaat and Zierikzee.

Where

Where you can see seals

Around Neeltje Jans

Zeeland.com mentions the area around Neeltje Jans as a place for seals. Neeltje Jans is about 10 km by bike from Wissenkerke. Keep a reasonable distance and bring binoculars.

Boat trip from Neeltje Jans

Neeltje Jans runs a boat trip with the Christiaan B, only in combination with a visit to the park. The standard departure time is 1.30 pm. Between October and April the trip can be cancelled because of storms. Booking is not possible.

Seal safari from Zierikzee

Safari Frisia Rondvaarten leaves from Het Luitje 6 in Zierikzee. The trip takes about 2 hours and is by reservation. Zierikzee is about 18 km by bike from Wissenkerke. Check the price and sailing times with the operator.

Other operators

Natuurmonumenten also runs guided seal safaris. As far as we know, no seal trip departs from Noord-Beveland itself.

Timing

When is a good time?

There is no fixed best moment. Seals mostly rest on the sandbanks at low tide, so ask the operator when a trip is most rewarding.

Zeeland.com mentions the Oosterscheldeweek in the second week of September. That is a good moment to see whether there are activities around nature and water.

For Neeltje Jans: between October and April the boat trip can be cancelled because of storms. So in the winter season allow for a chance of cancellation.

Respect

How to keep things calm for the animals

Keep your distance and stay on the paths or on board. Going onto sandbanks is often forbidden. RUD Zeeland warns people and sends them away, because the Oosterschelde falls under Natura 2000.

We have not found a fixed minimum distance. Research on the Roggenplaat shows that seals already react at a distance of 181 to 219 metres. That is not a sailing rule, but it shows that calm is important.

If you see a seal, do not come closer, do not shout and do not make noise. Do not feed anything and stay calm.

If you bring your dog, it belongs on a lead. That is general advice for vulnerable nature. You will find more about rules at holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland.

Combine

Combine it with a stay in Wissenkerke

Wissenkerke is in the middle of Noord-Beveland. Neeltje Jans is about 10 km by bike and Zierikzee about 18 km. So a trip by electric bike is quite doable. At Juultje an electric bike costs €25 for the first day and €15 per day after that.

You will find routes and distances at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

If you want to make a weekend of it, take a look at a weekend on Noord-Beveland for two.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where do you see seals in the Oosterschelde?

Zeeland.com mentions the area around Neeltje Jans. You can also see them on sandbanks exposed at low tide.

Where does a seal safari leave from?

Safari Frisia Rondvaarten leaves from Het Luitje 6 in Zierikzee. The trip takes about 2 hours and is by reservation.

Can you book the boat trip at Neeltje Jans?

No. The trip with the Christiaan B is only possible with a park visit, leaves as standard at 1.30 pm and cannot be booked.

How much distance should you keep from seals?

We have not found a fixed minimum distance. Keep well back, because in research seals already reacted at 181 to 219 metres.
