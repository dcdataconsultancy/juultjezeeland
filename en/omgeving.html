Around Wissenkerke

Wissenkerke is on Noord-Beveland, between the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer. Ideal if you love peace and quiet, nature, walking and cycling. The beach is 2.5 kilometres from our stays.

Tips

Things to do on Noord-Beveland

Cycling

Noord-Beveland is flat with plenty of cycle paths past polders, dikes and villages. Cross the Veerse Gatdam to Veere and Walcheren, or the Zeeland Bridge to Zierikzee. We rent out electric bikes: €25 per bike for the first day, then €15 per bike per day.

Walking with your dog

You can walk your dog all you like through the polders and along the dikes. Local rules for dogs apply on the beaches and can differ by beach and season. Check the signs on site.

Oosterschelde and Veerse Meer

Oosterschelde National Park and the Veerse Meer are close by. Ideal for walks by the water, sailing, paddleboarding and birdwatching.

Towns in Zeeland

Veere, Middelburg and Zierikzee are a twenty to twenty five minute drive away. Goes is a little closer.
