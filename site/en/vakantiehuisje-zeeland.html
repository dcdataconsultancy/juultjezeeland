Renting a holiday cottage in Zeeland: which region suits you?

Zeeland can be divided into six areas, and your choice of region shapes your holiday more than the cottage itself. If you want seaside resorts and crowds, choose Walcheren or the west coast of Schouwen-Duiveland. If you want peace and a cottage for two, look at Noord-Beveland. Below you will find the character of each region, when to book and what to look out for.

Which region in Zeeland suits a holiday cottage for two?

The answer depends on what you want to do during the day. Zeeland is small: most places are within an hour's drive of each other. That means you can sleep in a quiet region and make day trips to the busier parts. The descriptions below follow the division used by zeeland.com on the Zeeland islands.

Regions

The character of each region

Walcheren

A mix of lively towns, villages, coastline and farmland. Middelburg is the provincial capital, with monuments and museums. Domburg and Zoutelande are seaside resorts and Vlissingen is a port city. This is the region for anyone who wants plenty of choice, cafés and a town within walking distance.

Schouwen-Duiveland

The west coast is the summery hotspot of the region, with clean beaches and plenty to see. The island suits cycling, walking, watersports and nature. Zierikzee is known for its many monuments and Bruinisse is a fishing village.

Noord-Beveland

An island with a strong sense of island life: fields, villages and many polders, between the North Sea, Nationaal Park Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer. The Zeelandbrug and the Oosterscheldekering link the island with the rest of the country. This is the region for peace and space.

Zuid-Beveland

A peninsula with blooming dykes, meadows and orchards. Goes is the main place to shop, and Yerseke is known as the mussel village for anyone who loves seafood.

Zeeuws-Vlaanderen

Clean beaches, history and polder roads linking the villages. The area blends Flemish and Zeeland influences with a relaxed way of life. It lies across the Westerschelde, so expect longer journeys to the other regions.

Tholen

Together with Sint Philipsland, known for peace, nature and water. Mudflats and salt marshes are rich in animals and plants. The town of Tholen is partly still walled. Cycling and walking are the best ways to get around here.

Why is Noord-Beveland quiet?

Noord-Beveland is an island of mainly farmland and polders, and it has few large towns. It lies between the North Sea, the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer, so you are never far from water. That is why it feels calmer than a seaside resort.

Quiet does not mean remote. From Wissenkerke it is about 18 kilometres to Goes, about 18 kilometres to Veere, about 22 kilometres to Zierikzee and about 23 kilometres to Middelburg. So you sleep peacefully and are still quickly in a town. You can read more about the village at Wissenkerke and surroundings.

When should you book a holiday cottage in Zeeland?

The high season roughly runs from April to September. In those months prices are higher and the most popular weeks fill up sooner. If you want a cottage for a particular week in that period, book well in advance.

The low season is the rest of the year. Prices are lower and the beaches and villages are quieter. That is especially pleasant if you want to walk, cycle or simply stay at home with a book. You can read what Zeeland looks like outside the season at Zeeland in autumn and winter.

What should you look out for when booking?

These are the points that decide whether your stay is enjoyable.

  • Dogs: check whether they are welcome, how many are allowed and what it costs per dog per night. The beach rules also differ from place to place, so read the signs on site. You can read more about this at holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland.
  • Parking: ask whether there is your own parking space or whether you park in the street. In busy seaside resorts parking in the high season is often harder than in a small village.
  • Tourist tax: the amount differs per municipality. Check whether it is included in the price or added to the bill as an extra amount.
  • Cleaning costs: some providers charge these separately. When comparing, look at the total price, not just the price per night.
  • Minimum stay: some providers require a week or a weekend. With us you can book from 1 night.

Book direct or through a portal?

On a portal you can compare many holiday homes at once, which is handy if you have not yet chosen a region. If you book directly with the owner, you agree the rules yourself and know who you are dealing with. The price may differ because a portal can charge service fees. So always compare the total price and check the cancellation terms. You can book our places directly via the availability page.

Cottage in a village or on a holiday park?

A holiday park offers many facilities in one place, but you share the space with many other guests and the atmosphere is more that of a shared site. A cottage in a village gives more privacy and the feeling of staying among the locals. The downside is that facilities are not always nearby. For a couple looking for peace, a cottage in a village is often the better match. If you also want to know how an apartment compares, read holiday apartment in Zeeland.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which region is best for a holiday cottage for two?

If you want peace, Noord-Beveland or Tholen suits you. If you want seaside resorts, terraces and a town nearby, Walcheren or the west coast of Schouwen-Duiveland is a better fit. Because Zeeland is small, you can visit the busier places from a quiet region too.

When is the high season in Zeeland?

Roughly from April to September. In that period prices are higher and popular weeks fill up sooner, so book well in advance.

Is tourist tax included in a holiday cottage?

That differs by provider, and the amount varies by municipality. Ask in advance and compare the total price. At Juultje Zeeland, tourist tax is included.

Can I take a dog to a holiday cottage in Zeeland?

Only if the provider allows it, so check before you book. On the beaches the rules differ by place and in the high season they are often stricter, according to zeeland.com. On Banjaardstrand on Noord-Beveland dogs must be on a lead from 1 April to 1 October. Checked in November 2026.
