Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

Wissenkerke: things to do in and around the village

Wissenkerke is a quiet village on Noord-Beveland, between the Veerse Meer and the Oosterschelde. Here you will find an old windmill, a village with an eventful history of water management and plenty of room to walk and cycle. From Wissenkerke, Kamperland, Kortgene and Colijnsplaat are quick to reach.

Where is Wissenkerke?

Wissenkerke lies in the middle of Noord-Beveland, an island in the Zeeland delta. On 1 January 2023 the village had about 1,240 inhabitants. Since 1995 Wissenkerke has been the seat of Noord-Beveland municipality. Before that it was a municipality in its own right, together with Geersdijk, 's-Gravenhoek and Kampensnieuwland.

The Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer are both close by. The beach is about 2.5 kilometres from our stays. By bike you are there in no time.

History of Wissenkerke

The old village of Wissenkerke was swallowed by the water in the storm floods of 1530 and 1532. Only the church tower remained standing. That says a lot about the landscape you are staying in: the land has been won back from the water time and again.

In 1652 the area was embanked again. The present village was laid out afterwards with plots at right angles, and shortly after 1670 a new church was built. Later the harbour of Wissenkerke disappeared, because new polders moved the water further and further from the village. You can still see the straight lines of the plots when you walk or cycle through the village.

If you want to read more about the island itself, you will find the historical overview at Wikipedia on Wissenkerke.

Sights in the village

On Ooststraat stands the windmill De Onderneming, a round stone gallery mill. A gallery mill has a surrounding gallery, so that the miller can reach the sails. It is a fine landmark when you walk through Wissenkerke.

At Dorpsdijk 98 there are wooden barns that possibly date from the eighteenth century. The oldest houses in the village date from the middle of the nineteenth century. That fits the history: after the storm floods and the embankment being redone, building only picked up again late.

On the edge of the village are the Sophiahaven marina and a campsite. We deliberately say nothing here about shops, restaurants and events in the village, because that changes quickly. Feel free to ask us for current tips when you arrive.

Do

What can you do in and around Wissenkerke?

Walking

Nationaal Park Oosterschelde has its own walking route near Wissenkerke. You can read the details on the park's website. If you walk along paths in nature reserves, watch the signs: a path can be closed temporarily because of maintenance or hunting. Keep your distance from livestock and do not feed the animals.

Cycling

Zeeland has a cycle junction network and Noord-Beveland is flat. The Ontdek Noord-Beveland route is 36 km long and runs past Kortgene and Colijnsplaat, along cycle paths on the outer side of the dykes. You have views of the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer. With us you can rent an electric bike: €25 per bike for the first day and €15 per bike per day after that.

Water and porpoises

Wissenkerke is mentioned as a good place to see porpoises from the shore. There is no guarantee, but a look at the water costs nothing. On the Veerse Meer windsurfing is popular and there are surf schools where you can learn to surf, paddleboard, wakeboard or waterski.

Veerse Gatdam and Zeelandbrug

The Veerse Gatdam is 2.8 km long and was closed on 27 April 1961, after which the Veerse Meer came into being. The Zeelandbrug is about 5,022 metres long and opened on 15 December 1965. It is the longest bridge in the Netherlands, with a narrow cycle path next to the carriageways.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where is Wissenkerke?

Wissenkerke is on Noord-Beveland in the province of Zeeland. Since 1995 the village has been the seat of Noord-Beveland municipality and on 1 January 2023 it had about 1,240 inhabitants.

What is there to see in Wissenkerke?

A notable sight is the De Onderneming windmill on Ooststraat, a round brick stage mill. The village also lies by the water, with the Sophiahaven marina. Wissenkerke is also mentioned as a good place to see harbour porpoises.

How far is the beach from Juultje Zeeland?

The beach is about 2.5 km from our places to stay. You can cycle there, for example with a rented electric bike.

Which places can you visit from Wissenkerke?

By car you are in Kamperland in about 5 km, in Kortgene in 8 km and in Colijnsplaat in 9 km. Veere is about 21 km away, Zierikzee 22 km and Middelburg 23 km.

Can my dog come to Wissenkerke?

Yes, dogs are welcome in both places to stay, up to three per place. In built up areas your dog must be on a lead. Outside built up areas it may run free, unless signs say otherwise.
