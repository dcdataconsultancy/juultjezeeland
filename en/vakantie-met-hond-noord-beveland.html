Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

A dog friendly holiday on Noord-Beveland

A holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland is easy to organise: outside built up areas a dog may run free unless signs say otherwise, and our places to stay in Wissenkerke are dog friendly. If you travel from Germany, Belgium or the United Kingdom, you need a chip, a rabies vaccination and the right document.

Why does Noord-Beveland suit a holiday with a dog?

Noord-Beveland is an island of polders and dykes. Along the outer side of the dykes there are cycle paths and walking paths with views of the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer. That gives you plenty of room for long walks with your dog.

The rules are clear too. Outside built up areas your dog may run off lead unless signs say otherwise. Inside built up areas your dog must be on a lead. The municipality has no designated off lead areas. Some beaches and nature areas have extra rules, such as dogs on a lead at Banjaardstrand from 1 April to 1 October.

Want to know where you can take your dog to the beach and which rules apply there? You can read all about it at dog beaches on Noord-Beveland.

What do our places to stay offer for dogs?

Dogs are welcome in Holiday cottage Juultje and in Apartment Juultje. Up to three dogs per place are allowed. You pay €15 per dog per night.

Your dog may lie on the sofa and the bed if you bring your own blanket. That keeps the furniture clean and gives your dog a familiar spot.

The holiday cottage is about 35 m² and has a small garden with a wooden canopy and its own parking space on the drive. The apartment is about 26 m² and has its own terrace, with parking in the street. The holiday cottage also has a dishwasher and a washing machine. Both places have air conditioning and wifi. Always keep an eye on your dog outside.

If you have a question about your dog, you can ask Marc and Angelia in advance.

Travelling in

Travelling to the Netherlands with your dog

From Germany or Belgium

Your dog needs a European pet passport, a microchip with the date and number in the passport, and a rabies vaccination. The dog must be at least 12 weeks old at the time of vaccination and the vaccination must have been given at least 21 days before departure. The vaccination is valid for 1 to 3 years, depending on the vaccine.

From the United Kingdom

A British pet passport is not valid for entry into the European Union. You need an Animal Health Certificate from a vet. That certificate is valid for 10 days for entry, and for 6 months for travel within the European Union and for the return journey. You need a new certificate for each trip. Northern Ireland can still use the European passport.

Chip and vaccination

The microchip must have been implanted before or on the same day as the rabies vaccination. So plan this well ahead of your holiday with your vet.

Tapeworm treatment

For the Netherlands, Germany and Belgium a tapeworm treatment is not required. For the return journey to the United Kingdom it is required, 24 hours to 5 days before arrival.

What do I need to arrange if I stay longer?

If you stay in the Netherlands for longer than 3 months, you must have your dog registered with a vet. For a holiday with us this usually does not apply.

The requirements can change. So check the current rules with the NVWA or your vet just before departure. We have not been able to consult the NVWA ourselves, so do not rely only on our summary for the latest position.

Checklist for your holiday with a dog

  • European pet passport or, from the United Kingdom, an Animal Health Certificate
  • Microchip and a valid rabies vaccination, given in good time
  • Your own blanket for the sofa and the bed
  • Lead, long lead, poo bags and water bowl
  • Food for the whole holiday and any medication
  • Phone number of your own vet
  • Possibly a basket or travel crate for the car

How do you plan your days with a dog?

Plan walks along the dyke and by the water, and check the signs at each beach to see whether your dog may run free. If you want a day out on your bike, look at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

Looking for something for two? You will find inspiration at a weekend on Noord-Beveland for two.

Also available: renting an electric bike, €25 per bike for the first day and €15 per bike per day after that.

Sources and checks

Checked in November 2026. The entry rules come from Nederland Wereldwijd and, for travel from the United Kingdom, from the UK government. The information on tapeworm treatment comes from the UK government. The dog rules on Noord-Beveland come from the Noord-Beveland municipality.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are dogs welcome in your places to stay?

Yes, in both places. Up to three dogs per place are allowed and it costs €15 per dog per night. Your dog may lie on the sofa and the bed with its own blanket.

What do I need to travel to the Netherlands with my dog from Germany or Belgium?

A European pet passport, a microchip and a rabies vaccination. The dog must be at least 12 weeks old at the time of vaccination and the vaccination must have been given at least 21 days before departure.

And if I come from the United Kingdom?

Then you do not have a pet passport that is valid for entry into the European Union, but an Animal Health Certificate from a vet. That certificate is valid for 10 days for entry and you need a new certificate for each trip.

Is a tapeworm treatment needed?

Not for the Netherlands. For the return journey to the United Kingdom it is, 24 hours to 5 days before arrival.

Does my dog have to be on a lead on Noord-Beveland?

In built up areas, yes; not using a lead there can mean a fine of €110. Outside built up areas your dog may run free, unless signs say otherwise.
