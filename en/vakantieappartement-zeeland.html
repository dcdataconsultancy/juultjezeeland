Holiday apartment in Zeeland: apartment, cottage, park or hotel?

A holiday apartment suits two people who want to be outdoors most of the time and to sleep and cook in a compact, fully equipped place. If you need more space, a garden or several dogs, a cottage is usually the better choice. Below you compare the four options and read what a holiday apartment should have as standard.

What is a holiday apartment?

A holiday apartment is a self contained living space that you rent by the night or by the week. You have your own front door, your own bathroom and usually your own kitchen or kitchenette. The difference from a hotel room is that you cook for yourself and can do laundry if the stay includes it. The difference from a cottage is mainly size and outdoor space.

Comparison

Apartment, cottage, holiday park and hotel

Apartment

Compact and usually cheaper per night. You have privacy and your own kitchen, but little space and sometimes only a balcony or terrace. Parking may be in the street. For dogs, the owner's rules apply, so ask in advance.

Cottage

More space, often a garden or canopy and your own parking space. Prices are usually higher than for an apartment. It is the best option if you are bringing dogs or staying longer and like to sit outside. You can read more at holiday cottage rental in Zeeland.

Holiday park

Many facilities on one site, but you share the surroundings with lots of other guests. Privacy and peace vary as a result. Handy if you want facilities within walking distance.

Hotel

No kitchen and no washing up of your own, but you get service and do not have to do anything yourself. A hotel room often offers the least space. Always ask in advance what is allowed for dogs.

Who is an apartment suitable for?

An apartment suits a couple who cycle, walk or go to the beach during the day and use the accommodation mainly to sleep, have breakfast and cook an evening meal. You do not pay for space you do not use. That is also pleasant for a short trip, such as a weekend or a few nights.

An apartment is less suitable if you work from home, have a large dog that needs a lot of room or like to be outside for long periods. Then a cottage with a garden is more comfortable. If it is a short trip for two, an apartment can be the smartest choice. You will find ideas for such a weekend in a weekend on Noord-Beveland for two.

What is standard in a holiday apartment?

The basics are often not in the photos. Check this before you book.

  • Bed linen and towels: check whether they are included or whether you need to bring or hire them yourself.
  • Kitchen: ask about hob, fridge, coffee maker and crockery for two people. A dishwasher or washing machine is a bonus.
  • Wifi, heating or air conditioning: for a stay outside the high season heating is important, in summer cooling is pleasant.
  • Tourist tax and cleaning costs: check whether these are included in the price or charged separately on the bill.
  • Parking: your own space, in the street or in a car park, and whether that costs extra.
  • Arrival and departure: the times and whether you collect the key yourself.

By putting these points side by side, you compare apartments more fairly than by price per night alone.

Where in Zeeland are apartments often found?

Apartments are found in seaside resorts as well as in towns and villages. For seaside resorts think of Domburg and Zoutelande on Walcheren and the west coast of Schouwen-Duiveland, for example near Renesse. You are close to the beach and terraces there, but it is busier in the high season. You can read an overview of the beaches at beaches in Zeeland.

In a town like Middelburg or Vlissingen you are close to amenities. In a small village it is quieter. The advantage of a village is that you avoid the crowds and can quickly reach another region. You can read an overview of the regions in which region suits you.

On Noord-Beveland an apartment in a village is quieter than one in a seaside resort. From Wissenkerke it is about 18 kilometres to Veere and 23 kilometres to Middelburg. You can read what to do in the province at things to do in Zeeland.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is a holiday apartment cheaper than a cottage?

Usually yes per night, because an apartment is smaller. Always compare the total price including tourist tax and cleaning costs. At Juultje Zeeland the apartment starts at €72 per night in the low season and the cottage from €86.

Can I take a dog to a holiday apartment?

That depends on the owner, so ask before you book. At Apartment Juultje dogs are welcome, up to three per stay, at €15 per dog per night.

What is the difference between an apartment and a hotel room?

An apartment has its own kitchen or kitchenette and more privacy, so you can cook for yourself. A hotel room is usually smaller and offers service instead of a kitchen.

Where is the best place to look for an apartment in Zeeland for two people?

If you want a seaside resort and the beach nearby, look at Walcheren or the west coast of Schouwen-Duiveland. If you want peace, a village suits, for example on Noord-Beveland.
