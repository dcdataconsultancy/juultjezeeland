Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

Cycling on Noord-Beveland from Wissenkerke

From Wissenkerke it is about 5 kilometres by bike to Kamperland, 6 kilometres to Kortgene and 15 kilometres to Veere. Noord-Beveland lies between the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer, with cycle paths along dikes and through polders full of birds. You can rent electric bikes at Juultje.

The cycle junction network in Zeeland

Zeeland has a cycle junction network. You cycle from junction to junction and follow the numbered signs, so you can put together your own route without a map full of arrows.

We do not list here which numbers around Wissenkerke belong to which route. They change and we do not want to give you wrong numbers. Plan your route beforehand in a route planner or app. For routes, Visit Noord-Beveland refers to route.nl and fietsknoop.nl, where you can search for Wissenkerke.

The landscape helps. On a large part of the island there are cycle paths on the outer side of the dykes, with views of the Oosterschelde or the Veerse Meer. Behind the dyke lie inlagen, areas rich in birds where you cycle past without traffic.

Rides

Four rides from Wissenkerke

To Veere over the Veerse Gatdam

Veere is about 15 km by bike from Wissenkerke. The Veerse Gatdam is 2.8 km long and was closed on 27 April 1961, after which the Veerse Meer came into being. In the summer season a passenger boat sails between Veere and Kamperland, handy if you want to cut out a stretch. Check the sailing times beforehand.

Over the Zeelandbrug to Zierikzee

Zierikzee is about 18 km from Wissenkerke. The Zeelandbrug is about 5,022 metres long and opened on 15 December 1965. Next to the carriageways there is a narrow cycle path. When it is windy you notice straight away on the bridge that you are cycling above the water, so allow for that.

A loop past Kamperland and Kortgene

Kamperland is about 5 km and Kortgene about 6 km from where you stay. Together with Colijnsplaat at about 9 km you can make a round of the villages. Zeeland.com describes an Ontdek Noord-Beveland route of 36 km past Kortgene and Colijnsplaat, with cycle paths on the outer side of the dykes and views of both waters.

Around the Veerse Meer

For a full day there is the route around the Veerse Meer of about 60 kilometres, via Veere. Allow half a day to a full day and check the exact length of your chosen variant in a route planner.

Practical

Tips for the road

Ferries in summer

In summer a cycle and foot ferry sails between Kortgene and Wolphaartsdijk. We have not been able to check this year's fare and timetable, so look them up beforehand. We do not know whether a dog may come along.

Wind and dykes

It is often windy on the dykes. Plan to ride out with the wind against you and back with the wind behind you, so the last stretch home is easier.

Nature and paths

On walking and cycling paths in nature reserves a closure can apply temporarily, for example because of maintenance. Watch the signs, keep your distance from livestock and do not feed animals.

Mountain biking

Zeeland.com mentions mountain bike routes on Noord-Beveland, including Noord-Beveland Midden of 37 km.

Bike hire

Electric bikes at Juultje

You can rent electric bikes from us. The first day costs €25 per bike, each following day €15 per bike. With pedal assistance the ride to Veere or Zierikzee is quite doable, even if you have to ride into the wind.

Ask about availability when you book, especially in high season. If you are looking for an alternative, the Visit Noord-Beveland website has a list of bike hire.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How far is it from Wissenkerke to Veere by bike?

About 15 km. The route runs over the Veerse Gatdam, which is 2.8 km long.

Can I rent an electric bike at Juultje?

Yes. The first day costs €25 per bike and each following day €15 per bike.

Is there a cycle path on the Zeelandbrug?

Yes, next to the carriageways there is a narrow cycle path. The bridge is about 5,022 metres long and it can blow hard there.

Which junctions should I follow?

Zeeland has a cycle junction network, but we do not list numbers for routes from Wissenkerke. Plan your route in a route planner or app and follow the signs along the way.

How do I cycle to Zierikzee?

Over the Zeelandbrug, about 18 km from Wissenkerke. Allow for wind on the bridge.
