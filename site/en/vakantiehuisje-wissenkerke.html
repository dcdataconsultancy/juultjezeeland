Holiday cottage Juultje in Wissenkerke

The more spacious of our two stays: about 35 m² for two people, with a small garden and covered wooden terrace, its own parking space on the driveway, a dishwasher and a washing machine. The beach is 2.5 kilometres away and your dog is very welcome.
