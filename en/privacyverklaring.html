Privacy policy

Juultje Zeeland attaches great importance to protecting the privacy of our guests. We handle personal data with care and process it only for the purposes for which it was provided.

In this privacy policy we explain which data we collect, why we do so and how we handle it.

1. Who are we?

Juultje Zeeland
Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland
Zeeland, the Netherlands
Email: juultjeverhuur@gmail.com

Juultje Zeeland is responsible for processing personal data as described in this privacy policy.

2. Which personal data do we collect?

When you book accommodation with us, we may process the following data:

  • First name and surname.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Address details.
  • Arrival date and departure date.
  • Number of guests.
  • Details about pets, if applicable.
  • Payment details and booking details.
  • Any additional information you provide to us yourself.

3. Why do we use this data?

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Processing and confirming bookings.
  • Handling payments.
  • Sending information about arrival and departure.
  • Answering questions and requests.
  • Providing our services.
  • Meeting legal obligations, including administrative and tax obligations.

4. Payments

Payments are processed through our payment provider Stripe or through the relevant booking platform.

Juultje Zeeland does not store full credit card details itself. Payment details are processed in accordance with the security and privacy terms of the relevant payment provider.

5. Sharing personal data

We never sell personal data to third parties.

Data may only be shared with parties that are necessary for our services, such as:

  • Smoobu, for booking management.
  • Booking.com and Airbnb, for bookings made through these platforms.
  • Stripe, for payment processing.
  • Government authorities when we are legally required to do so.

Where necessary, we make agreements with parties that process personal data on our behalf to ensure safe and careful data processing.

6. Retention period

We do not keep personal data longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Data that forms part of our financial records is kept for the statutory tax retention period of seven years.

Other data is deleted as soon as it is no longer necessary, unless a statutory retention obligation requires otherwise.

7. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access and unwanted disclosure.

8. Cookies and website use

Our website does not use cookies itself; the Smoobu booking system may place cookies that are needed to make a booking.

9. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have several rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access.
  • Right to rectification.
  • Right to erasure, insofar as permitted by law.
  • Right to restriction of processing.
  • Right to object.
  • Right to data portability, where applicable.

Would you like to exercise one of these rights? Please contact us at the email address above.

10. Complaints

Do you have a complaint about the way we handle personal data? Please contact us first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) via www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

11. Changes

Juultje Zeeland reserves the right to amend this privacy policy when necessary, for example due to changes in legislation or in our services.

The most recent version is always available on our website.
