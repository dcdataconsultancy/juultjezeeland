House rules

We wish you a wonderful stay in Wissenkerke. To keep it pleasant and enjoyable for everyone, we kindly ask you to observe the following house rules.

Arrival and departure

  • Check in: from 3 pm
  • Check out: by 10 am
  • The key is available from a key safe. You will receive the instructions in advance.
  • Early check in or late check out is only possible by arrangement.

Occupancy and visitors

  • The accommodation may only be used by the number of people stated in the booking.
  • Extra guests or visitors are only allowed with the prior consent of the host.
  • Subletting or transferring the booking to third parties is not allowed without permission.

Quiet and noise

  • Between 9 pm and 7 am we ask you to respect the night rest of neighbours and other guests.
  • Loud music, noise nuisance and parties or events are not allowed.
  • We ask you to treat the neighbours with respect.

Smoking and pets

  • Smoking is not allowed anywhere in the accommodation.
  • Pets are welcome with prior consent and as agreed when booking, with a maximum of three dogs per stay.
  • We charge €15 per dog per night for pets.
  • You are responsible for your pets and for any damage or nuisance they cause.

Use and care

  • Please treat the accommodation, furniture, appliances and other contents with care.
  • Report any damage, defects or other problems to us as soon as possible.

Cleaning and departure

When you leave, we kindly ask you to:

  • leave the accommodation tidy and swept clean
  • Do the washing up and, in the holiday cottage, empty the dishwasher
  • empty the fridge and take any leftover food with you
  • put the key back in the key safe

Bed linen and towels

  • Bed linen, towels and tea towels are included in your stay.

Electric car charging

  • Charging electric vehicles is only allowed via the dedicated charging point and by prior arrangement with us.
  • Using ordinary sockets to charge electric vehicles is not allowed.

Liability

  • You are responsible for damage caused during the stay by yourselves, your travel companions or pets.
  • Report any damage or missing items immediately.
  • Juultje Zeeland reserves the right to charge for demonstrable damage or extra cleaning costs resulting from not following the house rules.

Not following the house rules

  • In the event of serious or repeated breaches of the house rules, we reserve the right to take appropriate measures.
  • In the event of serious nuisance, dangerous situations or illegal activities, the stay may be ended, in accordance with the applicable legal rules, without any right to a refund.

By booking you agree to these house rules and our terms and conditions.

Complaints and service

We want you to have a pleasant and carefree stay.

Do you have a complaint or a problem during your stay, or does something not meet your expectations? Please contact us as soon as possible, so we can resolve it during your stay.

Please report complaints as soon as you notice them, and within 24 hours at the latest. That way we can act quickly and find a suitable solution together.

You can reach us via:

  • Call or WhatsApp Marc: +31 6 1366 4239
  • Call or WhatsApp Angelia: +31 6 8119 9425
  • Email: juultjeverhuur@gmail.com

If a complaint cannot be resolved during your stay, you can submit it to us in writing afterwards. We aim to respond in full within 14 days.

We do our best to handle every complaint carefully, so you can look back on your stay at Juultje Zeeland with a good feeling.
