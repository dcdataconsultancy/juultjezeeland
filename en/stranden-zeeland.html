Zeeland

Beaches in Zeeland

There is no single best beach in Zeeland, because every beach has its own character. If you want wide sand and dunes, look at Domburg, Oostkapelle and Zoutelande on Walcheren. If you want peace, Banjaard on Noord-Beveland and Cadzand in Zeeuws-Vlaanderen are good choices.

Which beach in Zeeland suits you?

Zeeland has about 650 kilometres of coastline, and according to Zeeland.com the province has been known for clean beaches for years. Some beaches have play equipment, others have a pavilion where you can hire a sun lounger, and some are suitable for dogs.

From Wissenkerke you are at the beach on Noord-Beveland within ten minutes. For the beaches on Walcheren, Schouwen-Duiveland and in Zeeuws-Vlaanderen you drive for longer. The well known beaches by region are listed below.

Noord-Beveland: close to Wissenkerke

Banjaardstrand lies between the Oosterscheldekering and the Veerse Gatdam and is a good three kilometres long. According to Visit Noord-Beveland it has been one of the cleanest beaches in the Netherlands for years.

On the Oosterschelde lies the beach of Roompot Beachresort. There are also small sandy beaches and coves along the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer, for example near Colijnsplaat and Kamperland. At such beaches, check the signs and the water quality on site. You can read more about the inland waters on the page about the Veerse Meer.

Walcheren

Wide beaches and seaside resorts on Walcheren

Vrouwenpolder

A wide, clean beach on the north side of Walcheren, close to the Oranjezon nature area. You look out over the Oosterscheldekering. Surf lessons and stand up paddleboarding lessons are given here.

Domburg

A seaside resort with a wide sandy beach and dunes behind the village. The Badstrand lies northwest of the village. Surf lessons can be booked in Domburg.

Oostkapelle

A sandy beach on the North Sea, next to the Manteling van Walcheren nature area. There is a zone for naturism, so watch the signs if you come with children.

Zoutelande

A wide, clean beach with a dune landscape behind it. The village lies on the coast and has a small seafront promenade.

Vlissingen

On the edge of the port city lies Nolle beach, in two parts: the Badstrand and the Nollestrand behind the Nollebos. Handy if you want to combine the beach with a visit to the city.

Further away

Schouwen-Duiveland and Zeeuws-Vlaanderen

Renesse and the Brouwersdam

Renesse lies on the coast of Schouwen-Duiveland. This makes it a day trip, because from Wissenkerke it is about 30 minutes away. At the Brouwersdam it is all about wind and water. On the side of the Grevelingenmeer lies the Kabbelaarsbank, a well known spot for windsurfing. Anyone paddleboarding here shares the water with kitesurfers and seals, according to Zeeland.com, so keep a good distance.

Cadzand

Cadzand is the southernmost beach in the Netherlands and is often named as one of the cleanest. The beach is about 14 kilometres long, so you can usually find a quiet spot. Just across the border lies the Zwin. It is the longest drive in this overview, so plan a full day for it.

Good to know

Swimming, currents and lifeguards

Where can I swim safely?

Only swim in places where the water quality and safety have been checked. On zwemwater.nl you can see for each swimming spot how clean the water is and whether a warning or a swimming ban applies.

How do the flags and the lifeguards work?

According to Zeeland.com, beach flags are raised where lifeguards are present. If you see no flag, take extra care. Many beaches on Walcheren have lifeguards, but times and staffing vary. If you see someone in trouble, get the lifeguards or call 112.

What should I know about currents and wind?

In the sea you have to deal with currents, waves and the effects of wind and gusts. That is very different from a swimming pool. On the Oosterschelde and Westerschelde shipping is added, so do not go too far into the water.

Are there sharp shells or jellyfish?

Some spots have Japanese oysters. Their shells have sharp edges and can cause deep cuts, so wear water shoes. There are also small sticky jellyfish, under two centimetres long, which can cause itching and a rash. The GGD Zeeland has a leaflet on this.

Can my dog come to the beach?

Usually yes, but the rules differ per beach and per season. At Banjaardstrand dogs must be on a lead from 1 April to 1 October. Information signs show where and when dogs may run free, and in high season the rules are often stricter. Read all the rules on the page dog beaches on Noord-Beveland.

Sources: Zeeland.com on dogs on the beach and Zeeland.com on safe swimming. Checked in November 2026.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the most beautiful beach in Zeeland?

It depends on what you are looking for. For wide sand and dunes, choose Domburg, Oostkapelle or Zoutelande. For peace and quiet, choose Banjaardstrand or Cadzand. For wind and water, head to the Brouwersdam.

Which beach is closest to Wissenkerke?

On Noord-Beveland, it is about ten minutes by car to Banjaardstrand or to the beach at Kamperland on the Oosterschelde.

Where can I check whether the water is clean?

On zwemwater.nl you will find the water quality for each swimming spot, with current warnings. There you can also see whether swimming is advised against or prohibited.

Is there always a lifeguard service on the beach?

No. Beach flags only fly where a lifeguard service is present. If you see no flag, take extra care. Times and staffing differ per beach, so check on the spot.

Can your dog come to the beach in Zeeland?

On many beaches, yes, but the rules differ per beach and per season. The signs at the entrance are decisive. At Banjaardstrand a lead requirement applies from 1 April to 1 October.
