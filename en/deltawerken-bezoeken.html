Visiting the Delta Works from Wissenkerke

The easiest way to visit the Delta Works is at the Oosterscheldekering and Deltapark Neeltje Jans, about 15 minutes by car from Wissenkerke. The Watersnoodmuseum in Ouwerkerk tells the story behind the dams. Several dams you can view yourself without paying admission.

What are the Delta Works?

The Delta Works are a system of dams, locks and storm surge barriers. They protect Zeeland, the south of Zuid-Holland and Noord-Brabant against sea flooding.

The reason was the 1953 flood disaster. 1,836 people lost their lives in it. The Delta Act was signed on 8 May 1958. The project was completed with the Oosterscheldekering in 1986, the Maeslantkering in 1997 and the raising of the dikes in August 2010.

For a stay on Noord-Beveland this is particularly close. The island lies between two Delta Works: the Zandkreekdam and the Veerse Gatdam, and the Oosterscheldekering runs straight to it.

Visit

What you can visit

Oosterscheldekering

The barrier is 9 kilometres long, of which 3 kilometres can be closed off. The N57 runs over the barrier between Schouwen-Duiveland and Noord-Beveland. It opened on 4 October 1986. Rijkswaterstaat manages it.

Deltapark Neeltje Jans

The park is at Faelweg 5 in Vrouwenpolder. There you will find the interactive exhibition about the story of the Oosterschelde, a virtual reality experience under water and water attractions. Check opening times and tickets on the park's website.

Watersnoodmuseum

The museum is in Ouwerkerk, in the four caissons used to close the last gap in the dyke on the night of 6 to 7 November 1953. It tells the story of the disaster itself and of water knowledge. Check opening times with the museum.

Zeelandbrug

The bridge is about 5 kilometres long and opened on 15 December 1965. It connects Noord-Beveland with Schouwen-Duiveland. Alongside the carriageways runs a narrow cycle path.

See for yourself

Dams you can see for yourself

Not everything needs a ticket. A few of the Delta Works you can simply drive, cycle or view from the bank.

The Zandkreekdam is 830 metres long and lies between Kats on Noord-Beveland and Wilhelminadorp on Zuid-Beveland. It opened in 1960. Beside the dam lies the Zandkreeksluis, so boats can still pass through.

The Veerse Gatdam is 2.8 kilometres long and was closed on 27 April 1961. This created the Veerse Meer. The N57 runs over the dam. The lake is popular with windsurfers.

The Brouwersdam is about 6.5 kilometres long and was completed in 1971. Halfway along lies Port Zélande. There you can see kitesurfers on the sea side and windsurfers on both sides.

You can read more about the water around Noord-Beveland at the Veerse Meer.

Bike

By bike to the Delta Works

Neeltje Jans is about 10 kilometres by bike from Wissenkerke, so a round trip is easily done. At Juultje you can hire an electric bike: the first day costs € 25 per bike, then € 15 per bike per day.

The Zandkreekdam and the Veerse Gatdam are just over 14 and 15 kilometres away. For most people the Watersnoodmuseum and the Brouwersdam are a day trip by car. We have not confirmed whether you may cycle over the Oosterscheldekering, so look that up in advance.

You will find routes and distances at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

Combine

Seals and storms too

The Oosterscheldekering closes when the forecast water level reaches 3 metres above NAP at Roompot Buiten. Closing takes about 75 minutes. This way, in autumn and winter, you can see what the barrier really does.

Around Neeltje Jans you can also see seals. Everything about this, including boat trips and rules on distance, is at seal spotting in the Oosterschelde.

A day at the Delta Works fits well into a long weekend. You will find more ideas in the overview of outings in Zeeland.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are the Delta Works?

A system of dams, locks and storm surge barriers that protects Zeeland and the surrounding areas from the sea. It came about after the 1953 flood disaster.

How far is the Oosterscheldekering from Wissenkerke?

Neeltje Jans is about 15 minutes' drive from Wissenkerke, roughly 13 kilometres. By bike it is about 10 kilometres.

Where is the Watersnoodmuseum?

Weg van de Buitenlandse Pers 5 in Ouwerkerk, about 30 minutes' drive from Wissenkerke.

Can you see the Delta Works without paying admission?

Yes. You can visit the Zandkreekdam, the Veerse Gatdam, the Zeelandbrug and the Brouwersdam yourself. For Neeltje Jans and the Watersnoodmuseum you pay admission.
