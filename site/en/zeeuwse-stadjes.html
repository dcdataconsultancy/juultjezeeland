Zeeland

Zeeland towns for a day out

From Wissenkerke you can reach Middelburg, Veere, Zierikzee and Goes in about 20 to 30 minutes by car. Middelburg offers the most choice for a full day, Veere and Zierikzee are more compact, and Goes is the closest. Below, you can read per town what to see and how best to get there.

Which Zeeland town should you choose for a day out?

If you have a whole day, choose Middelburg: the town has more than 1,200 monuments and you can walk around all day. If you want half a day with a view of the water, choose Veere. Zierikzee suits anyone looking for harbours, town gates and a tower without the crowds. Goes is the practical choice if you want to shop or catch a market day.

All four towns are a stone's throw from each other, but you reach them via different dams and bridges. The distances and times in the table below are an indication from Wissenkerke. Roadworks or heavy traffic can change them, so check your route in a route planner before you set off.

The towns

Four towns in brief

Middelburg

Middelburg grew up around an abbey founded by Flemish monks around the year 1100. The town has more than 1,200 monuments, mainly from the Golden Age. The Lange Jan, the abbey tower, is 90.5 metres high and you climb it via 207 steps. On the Markt stands the town hall, built from 1452.

Veere

Veere was once a wealthy trading town with strong ties to Scotland. The Grote Kerk, dating from the fourteenth century, is a striking building for such a small town. Museum Veere is housed in the town hall and in the Schotse Huizen on the Kaai. Terraces on the harbour overlook the water.

Zierikzee

Zierikzee is on Schouwen-Duiveland and still has three medieval gates: the Noordhavenpoort, the Zuidhavenpoort and the Nobelpoort. On the Stadhuisplein stands the town hall with the Stadhuismuseum. The Sint Lievensmonstertoren rises above the town. Check in advance whether and when you can climb it.

Goes

Goes lies on Zuid-Beveland and has a medieval centre with narrow lanes and an old town harbour. There are nearly 300 shops, most in and around the old town centre. On the Grote Markt there are market stalls on Tuesdays and Saturdays. A steam train line also runs from Goes to Borsele.

A day in Middelburg: what should you do?

Start at the Markt and the Abbey, which lie close together. The Abdijplein is a green square between the buildings, and the Lange Jan can be seen from afar. On Thursdays the Markt is market day, a good time to visit if you enjoy a lively atmosphere. Check with the municipality whether the market is also held on other days and what the times are.

A walk of about four kilometres follows the bastions around the town. It is a peaceful way to see the old centre from the outside. For climbing the Lange Jan, check the opening times and prices in advance on the official website.

Parking: the old town centre is largely car free and the centre is easy to explore on foot. Follow the signs to car parks on the edge and check rates and payment methods on the spot.

Visiting Veere: what to see and how to get there?

Veere is small and you can walk around the centre in a few hours. A loop from the quay to the Markt and back, with a stop at the Schotse Huizen and a look inside the Grote Kerk, takes about an hour and a half to two hours. If you want to see more, walk to the Campveerse Toren from the fifteenth century, now a hotel and restaurant.

You can park on the edge of the town. According to the ANWB there is a car park at Polredijk 21, but check on the spot whether it is still free. Veere is also a good stop if you cycle around the Veerse Meer. You can read more about this at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

In summer a passenger boat sails between Veere and Kamperland. Check the timetable and whether bikes are allowed on board on the website of the ferry service.

Zierikzee: gates, harbour and tower

Zierikzee is mainly a town to stroll through its old streets and along the harbours. Walk from the Noordhavenpoort through the centre to the town hall, and at the Oude Haven and Nieuwe Haven watch the boats. For climbing the Sint Lievensmonstertoren, check the current opening times.

From Wissenkerke you drive over the Zeelandbrug, which is about 5 kilometres long. Park on the edge of the centre and walk the last stretch. For a trip out on the water from Zierikzee, you can book a seal safari, see seals on the Oosterschelde.

Goes: shopping and market

Goes is the most practical choice for anyone who wants a day of shopping or to visit the market. The centre, with its old buildings and town harbour, is easy to explore on foot. The Grote Markt is a spacious square, so in good weather the terraces make a pleasant place to pause. Check the current market times with the municipality.

If you want to travel by steam train, check the website of the steam railway to see whether it runs that day, as the trains only run in the tourist season.

Planning

Combining and other outings

Middelburg and Veere both lie on Walcheren and combine well in one day. Zierikzee is better suited to a day of its own, since you drive there over the Zeelandbrug. Goes combines well with a visit to the Oosterschelde.

You will find all outings in the region in the overview things to do in Zeeland. If you fancy tasting something typically Zeeland after your day in town, read Zeeland specialities.

If you also want to explore the island itself, you will find tips for the area at Wissenkerke.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which Zeeland town is the most beautiful for a day out?

It depends on your wishes. Middelburg is the largest and has the most sights. Veere is small and lies by the water. Zierikzee is quieter and has medieval town gates. Goes is handy for shopping and the market.

How far is Middelburg from Wissenkerke?

About 23 kilometres, around 30 minutes by car. This is an indication, so check your route in a route planner.

Which day is market day in Middelburg?

On Thursdays the Markt is market day. Check the times and any other market days with the municipality.

Can you visit Veere without a car?

Yes, for example by bike as a stop on a loop around the Veerse Meer. In summer a passenger boat also sails between Veere and Kamperland. Check the timetable.

Staying in Wissenkerke
