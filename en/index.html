Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are dogs welcome?

Yes, your dog is very welcome. Up to three dogs are allowed per stay, at €15 per dog per night. Dogs may go on the sofa and bed if you use your own protective blanket.

How far is the beach?

The beach is 2.5 kilometres from Wissenkerke, an easy bike ride. Please check the local rules for dogs on the beach.

What is the difference between the holiday cottage and the apartment?

The holiday cottage is about 35 m² and has a small garden with a covered wooden terrace, its own parking space, a dishwasher, a washing machine and a TV with Chromecast and Netflix. The apartment is about 26 m² and has its own terrace. Both are for two people.

Is there parking?

The holiday cottage has its own parking space on the driveway. For the apartment you park on the street.

What are the sleeping arrangements?

Both stays have one bedroom with a double bed and one bathroom. Extra guests only by arrangement.

Are wifi and air conditioning included?

Yes, both stays have wifi and air conditioning. Bed linen, towels and tea towels are included too.

Is the tourist tax included?

Yes, all prices include tourist tax. The cleaning fee is €45 for the holiday cottage and €40 for the apartment, per stay.

What is the minimum stay?

You can book from one night.

What are the check in and check out times?

Check in is from 3 pm, by yourself using a key safe. Check out is by 10 am at the latest. Earlier arrival or later departure only by arrangement.

Can we rent electric bikes?

Yes, the first day is €25 per bike, then €15 per bike per day. Please let us know in advance.

Can I charge my electric car?

Yes, a charging point for electric cars is available on request.

What if something is not right during our stay?

Contact Marc or Angelia as soon as possible, preferably within 24 hours. Then we can sort it out during your stay.

Are the stays wheelchair accessible?

Both stays have a low step, but are not fully wheelchair accessible: some passages and the space by the shower are limited. If you have specific needs, please contact us in advance.

Why book direct with us?

You book directly with Marc and Angelia, with no middleman. You are in direct contact with us and receive a confirmation straight after booking.
