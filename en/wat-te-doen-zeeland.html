Zeeland

Things to do in Zeeland

Zeeland is a province of islands, dykes and water, which makes it ideal for a short holiday full of outings: beaches, cycling, historic towns, the Delta Works and fresh oysters. This overview helps you choose and points you to a guide per topic. We write from Wissenkerke on Noord-Beveland, a central base for discovering Zeeland.

How do you choose outings in Zeeland?

Start with the weather and the distance. Zeeland consists of several islands and peninsulas, and you often cross dams and bridges from one part to another. That makes a day out rarely more than an hour's drive away, but a round trip across three islands in one day is too much.

A good rule of thumb is one main destination per day, with a short stop along the way. For example, combine a town with a stretch of dyke, or a museum with a beach walk if the weather clears. Below you will find the topics in blocks, each with a link to an extensive guide.

Overview

Outings in Zeeland by topic

Beach and sea

Zeeland has sandy beaches on the North Sea, the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer. Banjaardstrand between the Oosterscheldekering and the Veerse Gatdam is over 3 kilometres long. On the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer there are quieter beaches, handy for a quick dip or a walk by the water. You can read an overview at the beaches of Zeeland.

Nature and walking

Nationaal Park Oosterschelde has its own walking route near Wissenkerke. Watch the signs on the way: a path may be temporarily closed for maintenance or hunting. Keep your distance from livestock and do not feed the animals. You will find more ideas on the page about the area around Wissenkerke.

Cycling

Zeeland is flat and has a cycle junction network. The Ontdek Noord-Beveland route is 36 kilometres long and runs past Kortgene and Colijnsplaat, along cycle paths on the outside of the dykes. You can read descriptions of routes at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

Towns

Middelburg, Veere, Zierikzee and Goes are the best known Zeeland towns, each with its own atmosphere and historic centre. They all lie roughly half an hour's drive from Wissenkerke. You can read what is worth seeing in each town at the Zeeland towns.

Delta Works

The Delta Works are the dams and barriers with which Zeeland protects itself from the water after the 1953 disaster. Deltapark Neeltje Jans lies by the Oosterscheldekering, not far from Noord-Beveland. You can read practical information at visiting the Delta Works.

Food

Zeeland is known for mussels, oysters and bolussen. In Yerseke, in the heart of Zuid-Beveland, you can see where oysters are farmed and taste them. You can read which specialities you should know at the Zeeland specialities.

Watersports on the Veerse Meer

The Veerse Meer came into being in 1961, when the 2.8 kilometre Veerse Gatdam was closed. Windsurfing is popular there and there are surf schools where you can learn to surf, stand up paddleboard, wakeboard or water ski. You can read about all things on the water at the Veerse Meer.

Seals

Seals live in the Oosterschelde. Seeing them is not guaranteed, and you always keep your distance. You can read what else you can do at seals in the Oosterschelde.

When it rains

Zeeland has plenty to do indoors: a museum in caissons, an abbey full of art, a behind the scenes look at the Delta Works and a cinema. You will find our overview at Zeeland in the rain.

Autumn and winter

Outside high season Zeeland is quiet, with wide skies and empty dykes. Some attractions are then open for shorter hours or closed, so check in advance. You can find more tips at Zeeland in autumn and winter.

Planning

A weekend or a week in Zeeland

What can you do in a weekend?

Choose two destinations that are close together. A Saturday with a small town and a beach walk, plus a Sunday with a cycle route, is plenty. You can read an example for two people at a weekend on Noord-Beveland for two.

What can you do in a week?

Spread the week between water and land. Plan the sunny days for the beach, cycling and a boat trip, and save museums, towns and tastings for the rainy days. That way you do not have to force anything and you see Zeeland at its best.

Can I see Zeeland without a car?

On Noord-Beveland a lot can be done well by bike, because the island is flat. For Middelburg, Zierikzee or Yerseke a car is easiest, because then you do not depend on the timetable.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are the best days out in Zeeland?

That depends on the weather and your interests. Popular choices are the beach, cycling along the dykes, the towns of Middelburg, Veere and Zierikzee, the Delta Works at Neeltje Jans and tasting oysters in Yerseke.

How far is it from Wissenkerke to Middelburg?

About 23 kilometres, which is roughly 30 minutes by car. Check the current travel time with your route planner.

What can you do in Zeeland when it rains?

Visit a museum, an abbey, a cinema or a swimming pool. Zeeland has plenty, and most are half an hour's drive from Wissenkerke.

Is Zeeland suitable for a short holiday?

Yes. Distances are short, there is plenty to see and the landscape is peaceful. A weekend is enough to get a first impression.
