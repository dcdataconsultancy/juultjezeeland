Wissenkerke, Noord-Beveland

Dog beaches on Noord-Beveland

On Noord-Beveland you can take your dog to the beach, but the rules depend on the place and the season. There are no official off lead areas: in built up areas you always keep your dog on a lead, and elsewhere the signs on the spot apply.

What are the rules for dogs on Noord-Beveland?

Within built up areas your dog must be on a lead. If not, Noord-Beveland municipality can fine the owner or keeper €110.

Outside built up areas a dog may run free, unless signs say otherwise. The municipality advises using a lead if your dog does not come straight away when you call.

The municipality has not designated any dog exercise or off lead areas. There is therefore no official off lead beach on the island. Check the signs at each beach.

Do I have to clean up dog mess on the beach?

Yes. You must clean up dog mess straight away in built up areas, on beaches and in recreation areas. Only guide dogs and assistance dogs are exempt. So always bring plenty of bags, even for a long walk along the dyke.

What changes in the nesting season?

Between March and August, nesting birds need protection. The municipality asks you to keep your dog on a lead in that period when this is necessary or required. Staatsbosbeheer states that dogs may be walked on a lead in nature reserves unless signs say otherwise, and that a lead requirement or a dog ban can apply temporarily in the nesting season.

The exact dates differ per area. So always read the sign at the entrance, because that is what counts.

What about Banjaardstrand?

Banjaardstrand is the only beach on Noord-Beveland that Zeeland.com lists in its overview of beaches where you can go with your dog. From 1 April to 1 October dogs must be on a lead there. What applies outside that period is not stated, so check the information signs on the spot.

For the other beaches and small beaches, on the Oosterschelde and the Veerse Meer, we found no separate dog rules. Check there too what the sign says.

Practical

Tips for a beach walk with your dog

Before you set off

Check the signs at the car park or the beach entrance. They show which rules apply at that moment. Bring a short lead, a long lead, poo bags and enough water for your dog.

On the way

Keep your distance from livestock and do not feed animals. On walking paths in Nationaal Park Oosterschelde a closure can apply temporarily, for example for maintenance or hunting. A detour is quicker to arrange than an argument.

Afterwards

With us your dog may lie on the sofa and the bed with its own blanket. Dogs are welcome in both places to stay, up to three per place. You can read more about staying with your dog on the page holiday with your dog on Noord-Beveland.

Walking and cycling with your dog nearby

The beach is not the only place to walk your dog. The village itself and the dykes around it are well suited to a stroll. You can read more about the village at Wissenkerke.

If you want to go out on your bike and let your dog rest at home, you will find the routes at cycling routes on Noord-Beveland.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can my dog run free on the beach on Noord-Beveland?

That depends on the beach and the season. There are no official off lead areas. At Banjaardstrand your dog must be on a lead from 1 April to 1 October. Elsewhere the information signs decide whether running free is allowed.

What does it cost if I do not keep my dog on a lead?

In built up areas, not using a lead can mean a fine of €110 for the owner or keeper. We did not find a fine for not cleaning up dog mess, so we do not mention an amount.

When is the nesting season?

The municipality mentions the period between March and August. The exact dates differ per nature reserve, so read the signs at the entrance to the area.

Do I have to clean up dog mess?

Yes, cleaning up straight away is compulsory in built up areas, on beaches and in recreation areas. Only guide dogs and assistance dogs are exempt.

Can my dog come to your places to stay?

Yes, dogs are welcome, up to three per place. The cost is €15 per dog per night and your dog may lie on the sofa and the bed with its own blanket.
